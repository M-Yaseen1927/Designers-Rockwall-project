Established 2000

Crafting Timeless Walls With Mineral Elegance

Founded in 2000, Designers Rockwall specializes in taking ordinary architectural surfaces and transforming them into sophisticated statements of luxury. Our signature stone mist textures and house painting solutions merge organic earth tones with industrial-grade durability.

From opulent residential drawing rooms to high-profile commercial spaces like One Life Fitness and boutique design studios, our expert craftsmen guarantee flawless surface preparation and meticulous hand-applied detailing.
